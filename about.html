Our story

Started because good cleaning was impossible to book

In 2011 our founder spent six weeks trying to find a cleaner who turned up twice in a row. She stopped looking and started SparklePro with one rule that has not changed since: employ people properly, train them well, and the quality takes care of itself.

Every technician is on our payroll with holiday pay, sick pay and paid training days. That is unusual in this industry, and it is why our crews stay an average of 4.6 years and why clients get the same familiar face at the door.

Mission

Give people back their weekends with cleaning that is predictable, fairly priced and genuinely thorough.

Vision

Be the cleaning employer of choice in every city we serve, because well-treated teams clean better.
