How Deep Cleaning Improves Indoor Air Quality

  • Hana Sato
  • 28 February 2026
  • 9 min read
  • Healthy Home
  1. Home
  2. How Deep Cleaning Improves Indoor Air Qu

Indoor air is frequently two to five times more polluted than the air outside your front door. Most of that comes from particles that settle into soft furnishings and get re-released every time somebody walks past. Here is what is actually in a home, and what a deep clean removes.

In this article

  1. What settles in a home
  2. Why vacuuming can make it worse
  3. HEPA filtration explained
  4. Soft furnishings hold the most
  5. Humidity, mould and spores
  6. Products that add pollution
  7. What we measure after a deep clean

What settles in a home

House dust is a mixture of skin cells, textile fibres, pollen carried in on clothing, cooking grease aerosols, dust mite waste and outdoor particulates. In an average three-bedroom home roughly 18 kilograms of dust settles each year.

Why vacuuming can make it worse

A vacuum with a poor filter is a very effective dust distributor. It lifts particles from carpet and blows the fine fraction, which is the fraction that reaches your lungs, back into the room at head height.

HEPA filtration explained

A true HEPA filter captures 99.97% of particles at 0.3 microns, the hardest size to trap. Every SparklePro machine uses sealed HEPA filtration, which is why the air in a room smells different after we leave rather than just looking different.

“You cannot ventilate your way out of a dust reservoir. You have to remove the reservoir.”

Soft furnishings hold the most

Mattresses, sofas, curtains and carpets are the largest reservoirs. A mattress can hold several million dust mites and their allergen-carrying waste. Hot-water extraction and UV treatment reduce that load measurably.

Humidity, mould and spores

Above 60% relative humidity, mould begins to colonise grout, window reveals and behind furniture on external walls. Removing visible growth without addressing the moisture only buys a few months, so we flag the cause in the report.

Products that add pollution

Many conventional cleaners release volatile organic compounds long after the visit ends. Air-freshening sprays in particular replace one smell with airborne chemistry. Our range is fragrance-light and plant-based for exactly this reason.

What we measure after a deep clean

On allergy-focused deep cleans we record particulate readings before and after the visit. A typical result is a 60 to 80% reduction in PM2.5 within two hours of finishing, sustained for several weeks with regular maintenance.

Quick checklist

  • HEPA vacuum all soft flooring
  • Extract mattresses and sofas
  • Wash curtains and bedding at 60C
  • Wipe vents and extractor grilles
  • Keep humidity between 40 and 55%
  • Choose low-VOC products
